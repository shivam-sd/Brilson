# Brilson Website CMS

# Project Overview

Brilson is a comprehensive digital business card and NFC solution platform that enables business owners to create, manage, and share their digital profiles through NFC cards, parking tags, and QR codes.

# Key Features:

✅ NFC Card Management - Create, activate, and manage NFC cards
✅ Parking Tag System - Digital parking tags with QR activation
✅ Google Reviews Integration - Review management and QR generation
✅ Digital Business Profile - Complete business profile with products, services, and portfolio
✅ E-commerce Integration - Product catalog, cart, and order management
✅ Payment Gateway Support - Razorpay, Cashfree, and PayU
✅ Landing Page Management - Dynamic content management for website
✅ Admin Dashboard - Complete admin control panel
✅ Referral & Commission System - MLM and referral rewards
